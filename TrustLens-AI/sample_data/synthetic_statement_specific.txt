When I was fourteen, the only computer in Ropar's Government Senior Secondary School lab was a 2009 Dell that took six minutes to boot. I spent those minutes reading the manual for Turbo C. In 2022 I joined a two-month internship at a Mohali logistics startup, where I rewrote their route-planning script and cut average delivery time from 41 to 33 minutes across 120 daily orders. It was not elegant; my first version crashed every Friday because I had hard-coded the week length. My mentor, Harpreet, made me write a post-mortem, and that document taught me more than the fix did. Last year I built a Punjabi-English flashcard app for my grandmother, who wanted to read her grandson's school notices. Only 37 people use it, but she is one of them. I want to study human-computer interaction because I keep meeting software that assumes everyone reads English fluently and owns a fast phone. At your university I hope to work with the accessibility lab on low-bandwidth interfaces. I am not sure yet whether I want a research career or to start something of my own; I would like the next two years to help me decide that honestly. I failed my first attempt at the state robotics fair in 2021. Our line follower drifted left on the polished floor, and we had tested it only on our classroom's rough tiles. We came back in 2023 and placed fourth out of 58 teams, mostly because we finally tested on the right surface.
