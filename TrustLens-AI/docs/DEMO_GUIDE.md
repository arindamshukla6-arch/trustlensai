# TrustLens AI — demo guide for judges

All demo files are **synthetic**, generated by `trustlens/services/sample_data.py`. The checks run for real
on them; nothing is pre-scripted. The bands below are what the test suite verifies
(`tests/test_pipeline_dossier.py::test_demo_scenarios_produce_expected_bands`).

## Five demo test cases

| # | Scenario (in *Try demo case*) | Inputs | Expected result | What it shows |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Clean document | Unedited certificate PDF + specific statement | **Low** | No indicators; video and face checks listed as *Not run*, not suspicious |
| 2 | Edited certificate PDF | Grade covered with a white box, retyped, saved as an incremental update with a modification date before the creation date | **Moderate** | Findings with "can indicate / cannot establish" for each |
| 3 | Replayed video + edited PDF | A 2 s clip played three times + the edited PDF | **Elevated** | Flagged repeat timestamps on the frame-change chart |
| 4 | Poor-quality capture | Dark, blurry, 1.5 s, 176×144 video only | **Inconclusive** + recapture | Bad input never becomes "suspicious" |
| 5 | Generic statement (weak signal) | Unedited PDF + stock-phrase statement | **Low** | The AI-text indicator fires but is capped and warned |

Bonus: **Edited certificate scan** (JPEG with a pasted grade) → **Moderate**, with an error-level heatmap
that boxes the pasted region. Face matching: upload two consented photos of a team member (match) and of two
different members (mismatch) after running `scripts/download_models.py`.

## Likely judge questions

**Is this a deepfake detector?** No. Replay screening catches frozen and looped footage and the challenge
checks that a face moves on request. We label it "preliminary presentation-attack screening" because that is
what it is.

**Where does the face-match number come from?** OpenCV's SFace model produces 128-dimensional embeddings; we
report cosine similarity against OpenCV's published threshold (0.363) with a ±0.05 band treated as
inconclusive. If the model isn't installed we show *Not run* — we never fake it.

**How did you choose the weights?** By hand, for the prototype. They are in `.env`, documented as unvalidated,
and the dossier shows the exact weight × severity arithmetic. Real deployment needs calibration on consented
data and a fairness review.

**What about false positives on the AI-text check?** That is exactly why it is capped below the Moderate
threshold and every output carries a warning. Non-native and formal writing is frequently flagged by such
methods.

**Can the score reject someone?** No. There is no "rejected" status. `save_analysis` can only set
*Awaiting human review*, re-analysis never overwrites a human decision, and `test_automated_score_cannot_reject_candidate` proves it.

**How do you handle privacy?** Pseudonymous IDs, random storage names, content-checked uploads, sign-in for
evidence, aggregate-only dashboard, retention purge, per-case deletion and an audit trail.

**Does it verify certificates with universities?** No, and the app says so on every document result. That
needs an authorised issuer integration (e.g. DigiLocker / NAD in India), which we list as future work.

**Why Streamlit and SQLite?** A student team can run the whole thing on a CPU laptop with one command.

## Honest limitations
- Replay screening cannot detect deepfakes, face swaps, masks, high-quality screen replays or injected camera feeds.
- Very still candidates on noise-free cameras can trigger frozen-frame findings; repetitive scenes can resemble loops.
- Face matching needs the downloaded SFace model; without it, matching is *Not run*. With it, accuracy depends on pose, light and age gap, and has not been validated across demographic groups.
- Synthetic demo videos contain no faces (we ship no real people's images), so face presence is flagged in them.
- Digital signatures are detected but not cryptographically validated; there is no issuer database integration.
- Error-level analysis is easily defeated by re-saving or screenshots and can flag coloured stamps or logos.
- The statement-style indicator is a weak heuristic, not an AI detector.
- Weights and thresholds are unvalidated prototype defaults.
- Access control is a shared access code, not production authentication, and files are not encrypted at rest.
- Not tested for scale, concurrency, or accessibility beyond colour-independent badges and keyboard focus styles.
